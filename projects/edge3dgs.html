端侧 3DGS 训练加速器On-device 3DGS Training Accelerator

2025.11 – 至今present 系统架构 / 技术负责人System architecture / technical lead AMD Alveo V80 + 28nm

在单芯片上实现 3D Gaussian Splatting 完整训练闭环的端侧加速器，前向渲染、损失、光栅反传、几何反传与参数更新均在硬件上完成。这是团队目前规模最大的项目，本人负责整体系统架构与集成。 An on-device accelerator that closes the full 3D Gaussian Splatting training loop on a single chip, with forward render, loss, raster backward, geometry backward and parameter update all executed in hardware. It is the largest project on the team, and I am responsible for the overall system architecture and integration.

Sorting, however, accounts for only 0.62% of a training iteration against 95.9% for the render engine, so the end-to-end gain is close to zero. The value of this work lies in area (4.57×) and in removing a capacity ceiling, not in speed; presenting it as a speedup would be misleading.

系统架构与划分System architecture

训练流水拆成 7 个功能块：渲染引擎、排序、投影、硬件分箱、几何反传、Adam、损失。每块独占一路 HBM 伪通道，并运行在各自的时钟域，经 Versal NoC 互联。让每块跑自己的频率，是为了不让最慢的那个块把整条流水拖到同一个档上。 The training pipeline is split into seven functional blocks: render engine, sort, projection, hardware binning, geometry backward, Adam and loss. Each block owns one HBM pseudo-channel and runs in its own clock domain, interconnected through the Versal NoC. Giving each block its own frequency keeps the slowest one from dragging the whole pipeline down to a single rate.

块间不设直连，一律通过 host 中介式契约通信。该方案引入了一层调度开销，但使每个块可以独立迭代与独立上板；在七个块并行开发的条件下，这一收益高于所节省的周期数。控制面经 PCIe 汇聚至各块的 AXI-Lite；该版本的数据面仍需经主机中转，跨阶段数据在 HBM 与主机之间往返。 Blocks are never directly connected. All communication passes through a host-mediated contract. This costs a layer of scheduling overhead but allows every block to be iterated and brought up on hardware independently; with seven blocks under parallel development, that benefit outweighs the cycles it costs. The control plane funnels through PCIe to each block's AXI-Lite; in this revision the data plane still passes through the host, with cross-stage data shuttling between HBM and the host.

继而将主机层一并迁至片上Then moving the host layer on-chip

host 中介的开销终须偿还。主机职责现已迁往片上 Cortex-A72，ARM 裸机可独立驱动六个协处理器中的五个，其输出与经主机路径的结果逐字节一致。此处以逐字节一致而非「可运行」作为验收判据：迁移改变的是控制主体，任何数值偏差都意味着迁移并未真正完成。 The overhead of host mediation eventually has to be repaid. Host duties now reside on an on-chip Cortex-A72, where bare-metal ARM drives five of the six coprocessors unaided and produces output byte-identical to the host-mediated path. Byte-identity rather than "it runs" is the acceptance criterion here: the migration changes which agent is in control, and any numerical divergence would mean it had not actually completed.

数据面随之迁移后，两处等待各下降约一个数量级：引擎回读由 59.2 降至 0.2 ms/iter，分箱等待由 74.9 降至 2.5 ms/iter。二者原本均非计算耗时，而是数据在等待 PCIe 上板on board。 With the data plane moved as well, two waits each fell by roughly an order of magnitude: engine readback from 59.2 to 0.2 ms/iter and binning wait from 74.9 to 2.5 ms/iter. Neither was ever compute time; both were data waiting on PCIe 上板on board.

训练闭环的实现Closing the training loop

从零构建六阶段流水，并实现 C++ 主机训练器与 QDMA / CSR 运行时，支持仿真与上板双后端切换。验证采用逐算子 A/B 对拍，准入门槛设为梯度余弦相似度 > 0.99。训练场景与推理不同：单个算子的微小偏差会在迭代中累积放大，若等到 PSNR 层面才被发现则为时已晚。 The six-stage pipeline was built from scratch, together with a C++ host trainer and a QDMA / CSR runtime supporting both simulation and hardware backends. Verification used per-operator A/B comparison with an admission threshold of gradient cosine similarity > 0.99. Training differs from inference in this respect: a small per-operator deviation compounds across iterations, and detecting it only at the PSNR level is far too late.

ASIC 面积策略：以速度换面积ASIC area strategy: trading speed for area

整片面积由 SRAM 主导，占比在六成以上，因此优化重心应在存储而非逻辑：逻辑部分即便充分优化，可动用的也只是不足四成的份额。以下三条主线均由这一判断导出： SRAM dominates total area at over sixty percent, so optimization belongs in memory rather than in logic: however thoroughly the logic is optimized, it addresses only the smaller share. Three lines of attack follow from that assessment:

  • 有界化与溢出路径。片上缓冲按典型负载而非最坏情况定容，超出部分经 DRAM spill 处理，实质上是以访存往返换取片上容量。最坏情况出现频率很低，但为其常备的存储始终占用面积。 Bounded buffers with a spill path. On-chip buffers are sized for the typical load rather than the worst case, with overflow spilled to DRAM, trading memory round-trips for on-chip capacity. The worst case occurs rarely, yet the storage reserved for it occupies area continuously.
  • 时分复用折叠。前向与反向互为伴随，可以共享同一组 mode-switched MAC 阵列。综合数据显示折叠后面积减少约 65%，代价是周期数增加。 Time-multiplex folding. Forward and backward passes are adjoint to each other and can share one mode-switched MAC array. Synthesis shows folding saves about 65% of the area, at the cost of extra cycles.
  • 按实际速率 right-size。归并 FIFO 的深度按 K 路 reader 各自 1/K 的消费速率确定，而非按最坏情况。依据可能发生的情形定深度与依据实际发生的情形定深度，二者可相差数倍。 Right-size to the actual rate. Merge FIFO depth follows the 1/K consumption rate of each of K readers rather than the worst case. Sizing for what could occur, as against what does occur, can differ by a factor of several.

此外还做了两件小而有效的事：审计访问互斥性后把双端口 SRAM 改为单端口（以 6T 替 8T）；数值单元用 ROM 查表配合 Newton 迭代替代除法与开方 IP，以迭代延迟换取面积与 DSP。 Two smaller but effective changes: after auditing access mutual-exclusion, dual-port SRAMs became single-port (6T instead of 8T); and division and square-root IP were replaced by ROM lookup plus Newton iteration, trading iteration latency for area and DSPs.

落地：排序协处理器In practice: the sort coprocessor

上述三条方法均应用于排序协处理器。该协处理器实现逐 tile 精确排序（有界基数排序 + DRAM spill + 8 路流式归并），在算法未作任何修改的前提下将片上 SRAM 面积降低 4.57×。该面积并非估算值，而是按大宏的 MMC 实测数据逐个核算得出 综合 + MMC 实测synthesis + measured MMC。逻辑部分在部署频率下 WNS = 0 DC，FPGA 侧不占用任何 DSP Vivado。 All three methods are applied in the sort coprocessor, which performs exact per-tile sorting via bounded radix sort, DRAM spill and 8-way streaming merge. On-chip SRAM area was reduced by 4.57× without any modification to the algorithm. That area is not an estimate: it was accounted macro by macro from measured MMC data synthesis + measured MMC. The logic closes at WNS = 0 at the deployed frequency DC, and on the FPGA side it uses no DSPs at all Vivado.

附带的收益是：新方案在真实场景负载下达到 byte-exact，而该负载已超出旧方案的容量上限。超出上限后旧方案并不会变慢，而是静默产生错误的排序结果，且不触发任何报错。 A further benefit: the new design is byte-exact on a real scene's workload, and that load already exceeds the capacity ceiling of the old design. Beyond that ceiling the old design does not slow down but silently produces an incorrect ordering, without raising any error.

落地：几何反传单元与投影单元In practice: the geometry backward and projection units

几何反传单元采用时分复用：将发起间隔放宽 4×，浮点资源池随之收缩至约四分之一，单元面积下降 47.3% DC。这是明码标价的以速度换面积——发起间隔确实变差，但该级本非流水瓶颈，代价落在了有余量的地方。 The geometry backward unit uses time multiplexing: relaxing the initiation interval by 4× shrinks the floating-point pool to roughly a quarter and reduces unit area by 47.3% DC. This is speed traded for area at a stated price. The initiation interval genuinely worsens, but this stage was never the pipeline bottleneck, so the cost lands where margin exists.

投影单元的节省来自改写而非折叠：将协方差计算按结合律重排后消去了 21 个浮点乘法器，单元面积下降 43.2% DC；同时数值行为亦有改善，受误差影响半径的样本比例由 5.18% 降至 1.76%。改变结合顺序同时降低面积并提升精度的情形并不常见，值得单独记录。 The projection unit saves by rewriting rather than folding: reassociating the covariance computation removes 21 floating-point multipliers and reduces unit area by 43.2% DC, while also improving the numerics — the proportion of samples whose radius is affected by error falls from 5.18% to 1.76%. A change of association order that lowers area and raises accuracy at once is uncommon enough to record separately.

硅上问题定位Silicon debug

一处位宽解掉长期死锁One bit-width fix resolved a long-standing deadlock

一处硅上死锁长期存在，此前四轮 RTL 层面的修改均未奏效。当时的结论是 NoC 对协处理器读流存在硬性 beat 数限制。该结论在两个层面上都是错误的。其一，若干次所谓的复现实际读到的是陈旧数据：读通道卡死后软复位无法恢复，调试寄存器仍保留着上一次的快照，因此连续三次读取得到的是同一份记录。其二，在编写合成负载跳过前处理之后，单路连续读取上万 beats 未出现任何异常。 A silicon deadlock persisted through four rounds of RTL changes. The prevailing explanation was that the NoC imposed a hard beat-count limit on coprocessor read streams. That explanation was wrong at two levels. First, several of the apparent reproductions were in fact reading stale data: once the read channel wedged, a soft reset could not recover it and the debug registers retained the previous snapshot, so three consecutive reads returned the same record. Second, once a synthetic harness was written to bypass preprocessing, a single lane read tens of thousands of beats without incident.

真实原因是一个手写 wrapper 将 AXI ID 由 [6:0] 截断为 [3:0]，而归并单元的 run 编号恰好位于被截去的 bit[6:4]。其结果是多路 reader 共享同一 ID，仲裁器无法按 ID 路由；叠加 AXI 中同 ID 响应必须按序返回的规则，被阻塞的 reader 永久持有 outstanding 读请求，耗尽共享读资源并导致其余 lane 饥饿。 The actual cause was that a hand-written wrapper truncated the AXI ID from [6:0] to [3:0], and the run number of the merge unit fell precisely in the discarded bits [6:4]. Multiple readers therefore shared a single ID and the arbiter could not route by it. Combined with the AXI rule that responses on the same ID must return in order, the blocked reader held its outstanding reads indefinitely, exhausted the shared read resources and starved the remaining lanes.

将该字段位宽由 4 扩展至 7 后，死锁与读资源饥饿同时消除。此前四轮修改之所以全部失败，是因为缺陷位于 wrapper 的端口声明中，任何 RTL 内部的改动都无法触及。 Widening this field from 4 to 7 bits eliminated both the deadlock and the starvation. The four earlier attempts all failed because the defect resided in a wrapper's port declaration, which no change inside the RTL could reach.

面积报告里的黑盒Black boxes in an area report

另查出渲染引擎的 ASIC 面积报告将数百个浮点 IP 综合为黑盒，完全未予计入。修正后 DSP 少算 4.35×、LUT 少算 1.66×。重建网表之后方才得到可信的面积基线。此类问题不触发任何报错，只是给出一个偏乐观且形式上完全正常的数字，而整个面积收敛计划正建立在该数字之上。 Separately, the ASIC area report for the render engine proved to have synthesized several hundred floating-point IP instances as black boxes, leaving them entirely uncounted. Once corrected, DSPs had been undercounted by 4.35× and LUTs by 1.66×. Rebuilding the netlist was necessary to obtain a trustworthy area baseline. This class of failure raises no error; it yields an optimistic figure that appears entirely normal, and the whole area-convergence plan rested upon it.

协处理器设计Coprocessor design

Adam 优化器：把带宽问题识别成带宽问题Adam optimizer: recognizing a bandwidth problem as one

这一阶段的瓶颈在带宽而非算力。每个高斯包含数十个参数，每次迭代需经 QDMA 往返数百 MB 数据，而实际算术运算仅为若干次 FMA。将其移至片上的目的不是提高运算速度，而是避免数据搬运。 This stage is limited by bandwidth rather than arithmetic. With a few dozen parameters per Gaussian, each iteration moves hundreds of megabytes across QDMA against only a handful of FMAs of actual computation. The purpose of moving it on-chip is not faster computation but the elimination of data movement.

因此采用分块缓冲而非逐元素交错。逐元素交错会将一条顺序流拆成四条 strided 流，使 AXI burst 既短且乱序。此外由主机预先传入倒数，将两次除法转换为乘法。最终实现 II = 1 的满吞吐流水，消除 89% 的数据流量，且该单元的资源占用可以忽略。 It therefore uses chunked buffering rather than element-wise interleaving. Interleaving would turn one sequential stream into four strided ones, rendering AXI bursts both short and out of order. The host additionally pre-computes reciprocals, converting two divisions into multiplications. The result is a full-throughput pipeline at II = 1 that eliminates 89% of the traffic, at negligible resource cost.

Sobel 损失：把梯度通路压到两次浮点运算Sobel loss: down to two floating-point operations

利用两条性质将梯度通路压缩至仅需 1 次 FP 减法与 1 次 FP 乘法。其一，卷积具有线性性，Sobel(pred) − Sobel(gt) = Sobel(pred − gt)，故第一级只需计算一次卷积。其二，中间结果只取符号位，第二级的输入取值仅为 {−1, 0, +1}，而核系数为 ±1/±2，整个第二级退化为纯整数运算，无需任何乘法器。 Two properties reduce the gradient path to one FP subtraction and one FP multiplication. First, convolution is linear, so Sobel(pred) − Sobel(gt) = Sobel(pred − gt) and the first stage requires only one convolution. Second, only the sign of the intermediate result is required, so the second stage sees inputs in {−1, 0, +1} against kernels of ±1/±2, and the entire second stage reduces to integer arithmetic requiring no multipliers.

用冲激响应定位流水错位Impulse-response probing for pipeline misalignment

Sobel 模块初期始终无法对齐，遍历全部全局 shift 后最优结果仅为 26/256 匹配。后续分析表明，三处缺陷均为数据通路内部的错位，无法由任何输出 shift 表达。也就是说，shift 扫描一直在搜索一个并不存在的峰值。 The Sobel block initially failed to match under any configuration; sweeping every global shift produced a best result of 26/256. Subsequent analysis showed that all three defects were misalignments internal to the datapath, which no output shift can express. The shift sweep had therefore been searching for a peak that did not exist.

改用冲激激励后，硬件上的 10 个非零样本可由单一缺陷精确预测，既无残留也无遗漏，三处错位随之逐一定位。主缺陷是 a + 2b + c 被实现为两级串联加法，内层结果较 c 晚若干拍到达外层，致使每个列和的第三个 tap 取到了滞后若干列的像素。修正后在 6 类激励下达到 256/256 全部 bit-exact。 Under impulse excitation, the 10 non-zero samples observed in hardware were predicted exactly by a single defect, with no residual and no omission, and the three misalignments were then localized in turn. The principal defect was that a + 2b + c had been implemented as two cascaded additions, so the inner result reached the outer stage several cycles after c, causing the third tap of each column sum to read pixels several columns late. After correction, the block is 256/256 bit-exact across all six stimulus classes.

两条可推广的结论：其一，在不等深的加法树中，较浅的通路必须补齐延迟；其二，平坦或常数激励会给出虚假的通过结果，因为在所有 tap 相等时，tap 错位不可观测。 Two generalizable conclusions follow. First, in an unbalanced adder tree the shallower path must be delay-matched. Second, flat or constant stimuli yield spurious passes, since tap misalignment is unobservable when every tap is equal.
