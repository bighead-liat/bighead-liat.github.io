简介About

About 简介

我的研究方向是模型推理的硬件加速器（FPGA / ASIC），关注从算法层的量化方案到物理实现的完整设计链路：量化位宽与精度边界的确定、脉动阵列与 dataflow 的规模选型、Attention 数据通路的组织、稀疏性与访存的协同优化，以及上述决策在面积与功耗上的代价核算。

已完成的工作覆盖不同负载（神经渲染、前馈 3D 重建、混合架构大模型的批量解码），但其中的核心设计问题高度一致：稀疏性如何转化为实际收益、中间状态应当片上常驻还是回写主存、算术强度在 roofline 上的位置，以及面积与频率的折衷。因此这些工作并非彼此独立的方向，而是同一套方法在不同负载上的应用。

在校期间参与一颗 HLMC 28nm 芯片的流片，并独立完成该芯片的实测与深低温表征，最低测试温度 −167 °C。现于一家初创芯片公司担任加速器方向的技术项目负责人，在 AMD Alveo V80 平台上负责四款加速器的系统架构、RTL 实现、上板验证与 ASIC 面积收敛。

已发表 CCF-A 类论文 3 篇、CCF-B 类论文 1 篇，另有第一作者论文 2 篇在投。

My research concerns hardware accelerators (FPGA / ASIC) for model inference, spanning the full design path from algorithm-level quantization to physical implementation: determining quantization width and accuracy boundaries, sizing systolic arrays and dataflow, organizing attention datapaths, co-optimizing sparsity and memory traffic, and accounting for the area and power cost of each of these decisions.

The workloads addressed so far differ (neural rendering, feed-forward 3D reconstruction, batch decoding for hybrid-architecture LLMs), yet the central design problems are largely identical: how sparsity translates into a realized gain, whether intermediate state should remain resident on-chip or be written back to main memory, where arithmetic intensity falls on the roofline, and how area trades against frequency. These are therefore not independent directions but a single methodology applied to different workloads.

During my studies I contributed to a chip taped out in HLMC 28nm and independently carried out its silicon measurement and cryogenic characterization, down to −167 °C. I am currently technical project lead for the accelerator track at a chip startup, responsible for the system architecture, RTL implementation, board-level validation and ASIC area convergence of four accelerators on the AMD Alveo V80 platform.

Three CCF-A and one CCF-B papers published; two further first-author papers under review.
